### About Me

<p align="justify">
  I'm a Computer Science student at IBA Karachi who likes working close to the hardware, where systems programming and machine learning meet the real constraints of the machine running them. My focus is edge AI, computer architecture, and ML inference on small systems, from RISC-V assembly to Raspberry Pi clusters. I'm part of <a href="https://site-zeta-rust-98.vercel.app/">Systems Research @ IBA</a>, which I helped start, and I have been a teaching assistant at IBA for Programming, Computer Architecture and Operating Systems. Open to research collaborations.
</p>
